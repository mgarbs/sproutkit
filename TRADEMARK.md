# SproutKit Trademark Policy

The name **SproutKit** and any associated logos are trademarks of the SproutKit maintainers. They are kept separate from the code (MIT) and data (CC-BY-SA 4.0) licenses.

## What is allowed

- **Fork the code** under MIT. Build derivative works, including commercial ones.
- **Fork and redistribute the dataset** under CC-BY-SA 4.0. Attribution to SproutKit is required by the license.
- **Run your own deployment** of the MCP server with your own affiliate tags (see `apps/mcp/README.md`).
- **Refer to SproutKit by name** in factual contexts: "based on the SproutKit dataset," "compatible with the SproutKit MCP server," "originally forked from SproutKit."

## What is not allowed without permission

- Using the name "SproutKit" or a confusingly similar name for a fork, hosted service, app, or product. A fork that ships under the SproutKit name implies endorsement by the maintainers and creates user confusion about which version is canonical.
- Using SproutKit logos or branding in marketing materials, app icons, or domains.
- Representing your own affiliate-link choices, product recommendations, or data quality decisions as SproutKit's.

## Why this split

SproutKit's open licenses are deliberate: the data and code are meant to be forked, contributed to, and built on. The trademark is the one piece that can't be shared, because brand reputation is the only mechanism that distinguishes "the canonical, source-cited gardening knowledge base" from forks that may inject vendor marketing, low-quality SKUs, or unsourced facts.

This is the same model used by Mozilla/Firefox, WordPress, Mastodon, and many other open projects with a hosted commercial component.

## Questions

Open an issue or contact the maintainer if you're unsure whether your use case is covered.
